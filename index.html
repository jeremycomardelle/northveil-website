Built different

Every detail has a job.

Wildcat strips a music player back to what actually matters — and leaves out everything that doesn't.

8 buttons + scroll wheel

Tactile, click-wheel navigation built for muscle memory — skip, scrub, and browse without ever looking down.

Pocket-sized

Small enough to disappear in a pocket, armband, or gym bag — built for runs, lifts, and long drives.

Bluetooth 5.3+ & aux

Go wireless with the latest Bluetooth, or plug straight in with the 3.5mm jack. Your call.

Plays what you already own

Zero bloatware

No FM radio nobody uses, no half-baked apps, no notifications. A custom OS built to do one thing well.
